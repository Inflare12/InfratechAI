What it does

Inspection workflow, not just object detection.

The current project combines computer vision, tracking and reporting components into a local-first inspection workflow.

Image + video analysis

Analyze infrastructure imagery and video and generate annotated outputs.

Damage detection

Detect supported visible damage categories including cracks, corrosion, potholes, spalling and more.

Frame tracking

Associate recurring detections across inspection frames and retain timestamps and locations.

Severity engine

Current severity logic is damage-type-aware and geometry-based: Low, Moderate, High or Critical.

Reports & evidence

Produce annotated media, crops, timelines, statistics, heatmaps and PDF-oriented outputs.

Training pipeline

Includes YOLO/Ultralytics training, validation and inference tooling for future model development.

Technology

Designed to keep evolving.

The architecture supports local inference and future deployment to external infrastructure while keeping heavy model inference outside serverless frontends.

Computer vision

YOLO/Ultralytics-based detection and an inference service architecture.

Engineering-oriented outputs

Damage geometry, confidence, timestamps and inspection-level aggregation can be carried into reports and review workflows.

Extensible datasets

Training tooling is structured to support additional datasets, classes and future model iterations.
